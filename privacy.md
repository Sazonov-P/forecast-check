# Forecast Check — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Forecast Check is a browser extension that shows weather forecasts from several weather models and how accurate each model has been near you.

## What the extension stores

- **The place you choose** — its name and coordinates, either from the city search or, if you click “Use my location”, from your browser's location service.
- **Your settings** — units, models, toolbar and alert options.
- **The latest forecast and accuracy results** — so the extension opens instantly.
- **When to ask for a rating** — the date you installed or updated the extension, how many times you opened it, and whether you chose “Rate” or “Not now” when it asked once to rate it in the Chrome Web Store. This stays on this device and is never sent anywhere.

All of this is kept in your browser's extension storage. Settings may sync between your own browsers through your browser's sync, if you have it turned on. We have no server and no copy of this data.

## What is sent, and to whom

To get the weather, the extension sends requests directly from your browser to:

| Service | What it receives | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com, previous-runs-api.open-meteo.com) | Coordinates of your place or of the nearest weather station | Forecasts and past forecasts |
| Open-Meteo geocoding (geocoding-api.open-meteo.com) | The text you type in the city search | Finding places |
| NOAA Aviation Weather Center (aviationweather.gov) | An area around your place and the code of the nearest airport station | Weather station observations |

Like any website, these services see your IP address. Their own privacy policies apply: [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms), [NOAA](https://www.noaa.gov/protecting-your-privacy).

## Optional external resources

The Analytics tab contains links to the Free Weather Data Scorecard study on GitHub Pages. The study loads only when you choose a link, in a new tab. Each link includes the same static campaign labels for everyone and a label for the selected regional edition; it does not include your chosen city, coordinates, settings or a user identifier. These labels are not a click collector: the extension sends no usage events.

When you open a link, the destination host receives the request and your IP address as with any website. Its privacy policy applies: [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## When you remove the extension

Your browser opens a short page on this site, [sazonov-p.github.io/forecast-check/uninstalled](https://sazonov-p.github.io/forecast-check/uninstalled). Its address contains only the extension version and your browser's interface language — no place, coordinates, settings or identifiers. The page asks why you removed the extension and links to an optional anonymous survey (Google Forms; [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies) or to our email address. Answering is up to you. GitHub Pages, which hosts this site, receives the request and your IP address as with any website.

## What we don't do

- No accounts, no usage analytics, no ads, no tracking in the extension.
- We don't sell or share your data, and we don't use it for anything other than showing the weather.

## Your choices

Remove the extension, or clear its data in your browser's extension settings, to delete everything it stored.
