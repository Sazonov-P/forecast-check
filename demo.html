Forecast CheckAdd to Chrome

From your city to a checked forecast

A 25-second walkthrough: choose your city, see which forecast model had the smallest recent temperature error nearby, then read your forecast.

Version 0.1.4 is available in Chrome Web Store. This demo shows city selection, the accuracy comparison, your forecast and the feedback button.

Download MP4Download GIF
  1. Choose a city

    Search for a place in Settings.

  2. Check the evidence

    Compare past forecasts with nearby airport weather observations. Lower temperature error is better.

  3. Read your forecast

    Automatic selection uses the model with the lowest recent error for the main forecast.

  4. Send feedback

    Describe a problem or idea, then send it using your email app. Available in all ten interface languages.

Real app screens, captured 11 October 2026. London City airport (EGLC), 325 matched hours in a 14-day comparison. This demo is a sequence of screens with on-screen explanations, without audio.

Past temperature accuracy does not guarantee future accuracy. Results depend on your location, available observations and the comparison period.
