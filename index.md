# Forecast Check

A Chrome extension that shows the weather forecast from nine models — ECMWF IFS, the AI model ECMWF AIFS, GFS, ICON, Météo-France, UK Met Office, GEM, JMA and the Open-Meteo blend — and checks each of them against the nearest airport weather station. Your main forecast comes from the model that has been most accurate where you live.
